# Internal Linking & Content Architecture Case Study 🔗

A practical internal linking project for a travel website serving Inbound and Domestic tourism markets.

The project focused on improving relationships between destination content, travel guides, supporting articles, and commercial tour pages through contextual internal linking and topic-based content architecture.

# 🎯 Project Objective

The objective was to create a clearer internal linking structure across the website.

The project focused on:

- Connecting related content
- Strengthening important commercial pages
- Improving navigation between related topics
- Supporting topic clusters
- Improving anchor-text relevance
- Reducing isolated pages
- Creating clearer relationships between informational and commercial content

---

# ⚠️ Initial Challenges

A large travel website can contain many different content types.

Without a consistent internal linking strategy, common problems can include:

- Important pages receiving insufficient internal links
- Supporting articles not connected to relevant commercial pages
- Weak relationships between destination topics
- Generic or inconsistent anchor text
- Pages becoming difficult to discover
- Excessive links without clear contextual relevance

The solution was to approach internal linking as part of the website's information architecture.
