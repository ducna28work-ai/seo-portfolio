## ⚠️ Key SEO Challenges

The project involved several common challenges found in large travel websites.

### 1. Different Search Intents

Travel keywords can represent very different user intentions.

For example:

- Informational intent
- Commercial investigation
- Transactional intent
- Destination research
- Tour/product searches

A major part of the work was determining which type of page should target each search intent.

---

### 2. Large and Diverse Content Structure

The website covered multiple destinations, products, services, and informational topics.

Without a clear content architecture, this can create:

- Overlapping topics
- Keyword cannibalization
- Weak topical relationships
- Difficult navigation
- Inefficient internal linking

---

### 3. Product Pages Required Both SEO and Conversion Optimization

Tour pages need to satisfy search engines while also helping users make decisions.

Optimization therefore considered both:

- Organic search relevance
- User experience
- Information completeness
- Conversion paths

---

### 4. Technical SEO and Indexation

Technical analysis focused on issues that could affect:

- Crawling
- Indexation
- Canonicalization
- Duplicate URLs
- Site structure
- Internal links
- Search visibility

Google Search Console and crawling tools were used to identify areas requiring attention.

# 🔎 SEO Research & Strategy

## Keyword Research

Keyword research was used to identify opportunities across different stages of the travel customer journey.

The process included:

1. Collecting seed keywords
2. Expanding keyword variations
3. Understanding search intent
4. Grouping related queries
5. Identifying destination and product themes
6. Mapping keywords to appropriate pages
7. Reviewing potential keyword cannibalization
8. Prioritizing pages based on business relevance
