## 🔄 Campaign Workflow

**Business Objective → Keyword Research → Search Intent → Campaign Structure → Ad Groups → Ad Copy → Landing Page → Conversion Tracking → Search Term Analysis → Optimization → Monitoring**

---

## 1. Campaign Planning

Campaign structure was planned around the business objective and the specific travel products or destinations being promoted.

Planning considerations included:

- Campaign objective
- Target destination or product
- Geographic targeting
- Search intent
- Keyword themes
- Landing page relevance
- Conversion requirements

The structure was designed to keep targeting, messaging, and landing pages aligned.

---

## 2. Keyword Research & Search Intent

Keyword research focused on identifying queries relevant to the target travel offering.

The process included:

- Seed keyword research
- Keyword expansion
- Search intent classification
- Destination-based keyword grouping
- Commercial and transactional query identification
- Search term analysis
- Negative keyword identification

Search intent was used to determine whether a keyword was appropriate for the campaign and which ad group it should belong to.

---

## 3. Campaign & Ad Group Structure

Keywords were grouped according to their search intent and topic relevance.

A practical structure considers:

- Campaign theme
- Destination or product
- Keyword group
- Search intent
- Ad relevance
- Landing page relevance

The objective is to maintain a logical relationship between the user's search, the ad, and the landing page.

---

## 4. Search Ad Copy

Ad copy was developed around the user's search intent and the value of the promoted travel product.

Key considerations included:

- Relevance to the keyword theme
- Clear messaging
- Destination or product information
- Relevant benefits
- Appropriate calls to action
- Consistency with the landing page

Ad messaging should accurately reflect the offer rather than making unsupported claims.

---

## 5. Negative Keyword Management

Search terms were reviewed to identify queries that were not relevant to the campaign objective.

Potential negative keyword categories included:

- Irrelevant destinations
- Unrelated travel services
- Research-only queries where appropriate
- Jobs and recruitment searches
- Free or unrelated resource searches
- Other low-relevance queries

Negative keyword decisions were based on actual search behavior and campaign objectives.

---

## 6. Landing Page Alignment

The landing page was reviewed as part of the advertising workflow rather than treated as a separate task.

Key areas included:

- Keyword relevance
- Search intent alignment
- Content clarity
- Product information
- CTA visibility
- Mobile usability
- Conversion path
- Form or inquiry experience

The objective was to maintain consistency from:

**Search Query → Ad → Landing Page → User Action**

---

## 7. Conversion Tracking

Conversion tracking was used to measure meaningful user actions generated through the campaign.

The tracking workflow connected:

**User Action → Google Tag Manager → GA4 Event → Key Event → Performance Analysis**

Tracked actions may include relevant website interactions such as:

- Contact form submissions
- Inquiry actions
- CTA interactions
- Other defined conversion events

Tracking implementation was validated before using conversion data for campaign analysis.

---

## 8. Search Term Analysis

Search term data was reviewed to understand how users actually reached the ads.

The analysis considered:

- Search relevance
- New keyword opportunities
- Irrelevant queries
- Negative keyword opportunities
- Search intent patterns
- Ad group relevance

This helped refine keyword targeting and improve campaign structure over time.

---

## 9. Campaign Optimization

Optimization decisions were based on campaign data and search behavior.

Potential optimization areas included:

- Keyword targeting
- Match type strategy
- Negative keywords
- Ad copy
- Ad group structure
- Landing page alignment
- Conversion tracking
- Search terms
- Budget allocation within campaign requirements

Changes were reviewed after implementation rather than making isolated adjustments without measurement.

---

## 10. Performance Monitoring

Campaign performance was monitored using relevant advertising and conversion signals.

Depending on the campaign objective, these may include:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversions
- Conversion rate
- Search terms
- Cost per conversion

Performance should be interpreted in relation to the campaign objective, targeting, landing page, and tracking setup.

---

## 🧭 Practical Optimization Process

A typical workflow is:

**Plan → Research → Structure → Launch → Track → Analyze Search Terms → Optimize → Validate → Monitor → Iterate**

Google Ads optimization is treated as an ongoing process rather than a one-time campaign setup.
